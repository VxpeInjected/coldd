About coldd

A Roblox and Minecraft studio — scripting, building, UI, and VFX, all in-house. We make the templates, hubs, and systems behind games people actually play.

We make the parts other studios buy.

coldd started the way most good things do — a few people in a Discord, trading builds late at night because what was for sale just wasn't good enough.

That bar never moved. Today we're a full studio shipping 800+ Roblox assets and a fast-growing Minecraft catalog, with work running inside 100k+ creators' games. Scripting, building, UI, and VFX — all handled in-house, by the same people who started it.

We build what we'd actually want to buy, deliver it the second you check out, and stick around when you need a hand. No outsourcing, no filler, no disappearing after the sale.
